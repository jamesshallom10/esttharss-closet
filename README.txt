ESTHARSS CLOSET — STARTER WEBSITE

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
3. Internet is needed for the sample product photographs.

WHAT WORKS
- Responsive storefront layout
- Category filters and product search
- Add-to-bag, quantity controls, subtotal
- Cart is saved in the browser using local storage

BEFORE ACCEPTING REAL ORDERS
1. Replace sample product names, descriptions, prices and image URLs in the `products` array in index.html.
2. WhatsApp ordering is preconfigured to +234 813 136 1701. Contact email is jamesshallon10@gmail.com.
3. Add your delivery, returns and contact information.

IMPORTANT
This is a functional front-end starter, not a fully deployed or payment-enabled store. Product prices and stock are illustrative placeholders and must be replaced before launch.
